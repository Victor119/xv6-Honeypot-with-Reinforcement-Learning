The agent classifies the shell commands as follows: allow, block, and

Block (severity 3–4): Commands presenting high or critical risk, including commands capable of executing arbitrary code, compromising system integrity, or 
causing significant damage to the environment.

Conditional: Commands causing limited damage, permitted to maintain realistic interaction and gather behavioral data.

Allow: Non-dangerous commands.

The agent must balance system preservation against prolonged engagement.
